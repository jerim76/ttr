FAQS

Frequently asked questions.

Are the lessons one-on-one? +

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre provides individual one-on-one online tuition. Each lesson is focused on one student and their specific academic needs.

Are your classes physical or online? +

Our tuition is 100% virtual. Students connect with their tutors through live online lessons. We do not operate group physical classes.

Which curricula do you support? +

We support Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, International Baccalaureate, OxfordAQA International, AQA, OCR and WJEC / Eduqas, depending on the subject and level.

Do you support homeschooling? +

Yes. We provide online academic support for students following homeschooling programmes.

How do I enquire about tuition? +

You can call us, contact us through WhatsApp, send an email or complete the enquiry form below.

What information should I provide? +

Please provide the student's level, subject, curriculum or exam board where relevant, and the type of support required.
